## 1. Introduction

Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai (SRSS) recognises that the protection of personal information, particularly information relating to children, is an essential responsibility of every educational institution.

The SRSS Digital Hub is developed under the auspices of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) and operated for the educational and administrative benefit of SRSS.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, accessed, disclosed, protected, retained, and otherwise processed through the Platform. It applies to visitors, prospective pupils and families, enrolled pupils, parents, guardians, teachers, employees, volunteers, alumni, and other authorised users.

The School is committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently, and in accordance with applicable Malaysian laws. This includes the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709), as amended, where applicable, and relevant educational and safeguarding obligations.

Because the Platform serves primary-school pupils, additional care is required when processing children’s information. The principles of necessity, proportionality, confidentiality, accountability, and the best interests of the child shall guide the School’s approach to information management.

## 2. Institutional Responsibilities for Personal Data

The SRSS Digital Hub involves several institutional parties with distinct responsibilities.

### 2.1 Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia

SSIOM is the parent organisation associated with SRSS and the designated owner of the Platform’s original software and digital infrastructure, subject to applicable rights and development arrangements. SSIOM may provide authorised technical development, maintenance, and operational support.

Ownership of Platform software does not automatically make SSIOM the sole data controller or data user for all personal information processed through the Platform.

### 2.2 Sekolah Rendah Sathya Berhad

Sekolah Rendah Sathya Berhad (Registration No. 263453-U) is the registered governing entity responsible for SRSS. It provides the institutional governance structure within which the School’s educational and administrative responsibilities are undertaken.

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, the precise statutory and contractual responsibilities of the governing entity, SSIOM, and relevant service providers shall be determined according to their actual roles in deciding how and why personal information is processed.

### 2.3 Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai

SRSS manages the day-to-day educational and administrative use of personal information through its Principal and authorised school personnel.

The Principal serves as the designated operational contact for Platform-related privacy matters. The School has appointed its Senior Assistant for Student Affairs as Data Protection Officer, supported by a Deputy Data Protection Officer (the School Data Teacher). The Data Protection Officer handles day-to-day data protection matters under the Principal’s supervision.

Where applicable law requires the appointment of a Data Protection Officer to be notified to or registered with the Personal Data Protection Commissioner, the responsible entity shall do so.

### 2.4 Technical Service Providers

Authorised developers, hosting providers, cloud service providers, and other technical personnel may process information only within the scope of their legitimate responsibilities and applicable agreements.

Technical access must be appropriately restricted and must not be used for unrelated personal, commercial, or organisational purposes.

## 3. Categories of Personal Information Collected

The Platform may collect different categories of information according to the user’s relationship with the School and the services used.

### 3.1 Pupil Information

This may include:

- Full name and preferred name.
- Student identification number.
- Date of birth.
- Gender, where required.
- Nationality and relevant identification information.
- Enrolment and admission details.
- Academic year, Standard, and class.
- School attendance.
- Academic assessments and examination results.
- Teacher comments and progress reports.
- Learning portfolios and achievements.
- Co-curricular participation.
- Educational support records.
- Relevant safeguarding and welfare information.
- Photographs, recordings, and student-created materials.
- Other information reasonably required for educational administration.

The School should avoid collecting information that is unnecessary for its legitimate educational purposes.

### 3.2 Parent and Guardian Information

This may include:

- Full name.
- Relationship to the pupil.
- Contact details.
- Residential or correspondence address.
- Relevant identification details where required.
- Emergency contact information.
- Authorised collection arrangements.
- Consent and authorisation records.
- Communications with the School.
- Relevant administrative and payment information.

Information concerning custody or guardianship may be collected where necessary to determine lawful access or protect a child’s welfare.

### 3.3 Staff Information

This may include:

- Identity and contact details.
- Employment and professional information.
- Assigned responsibilities.
- Account credentials and permissions.
- Attendance and administrative records.
- Training and professional development information.
- Other employment-related records required for legitimate institutional purposes.

Confidential employment records must be subject to appropriate restrictions.

### 3.4 Prospective Families and Public Enquiries

Visitors who submit enquiries may provide:

- Name.
- Contact details.
- Child’s proposed admission year or Standard.
- Enquiry details.
- Appointment or school visit preferences.
- Other voluntarily submitted information.

The School should not request extensive child-related information at the preliminary enquiry stage unless reasonably necessary.

### 3.5 Technical and Usage Information

The Platform may collect technical information such as:

- Login activity.
- Account identifiers.
- Device and browser information.
- IP addresses.
- Access timestamps.
- Security events.
- Error logs.
- Session information.
- Cookie or similar technology identifiers.

Such information may be used for authentication, troubleshooting, security, service improvement, and legitimate operational purposes.

## 4. Sensitive and Confidential Information

Certain information requires enhanced protection because of its nature or the potential consequences of unauthorised disclosure. Examples include:

- Medical conditions and health information.
- Disability or special educational needs information.
- Safeguarding reports.
- Behavioural incidents involving sensitive circumstances.
- Family or custody disputes.
- Financial assistance information.
- Counselling or welfare records.
- Information revealing religious beliefs where collected.
- Other legally recognised sensitive personal data.

Access to such information must be limited to authorised personnel with a legitimate need to know. Sensitive information must not be displayed in general pupil profiles or shared through ordinary communication channels unless specifically authorised and appropriate.

The School shall apply relevant legal requirements concerning sensitive personal data, including consent requirements and applicable statutory exceptions.

## 5. Purposes of Personal Data Processing

Personal information may be processed for legitimate purposes connected with the School’s activities. These may include:

1. Managing admissions and enrolment.
2. Maintaining pupil and family records.
3. Delivering teaching and learning services.
4. Recording attendance and academic progress.
5. Preparing educational assessments and reports.
6. Supporting pupil wellbeing and safeguarding.
7. Communicating with parents and guardians.
8. Managing school events and activities.
9. Recording parental consent.
10. Administering school fees and related financial matters.
11. Managing staff responsibilities and access permissions.
12. Maintaining information security.
13. Complying with legal and regulatory requirements.
14. Supporting approved educational research or institutional reporting, subject to appropriate safeguards.
15. Maintaining historical educational records where legitimately required.

Information must not be repurposed for unrelated activities without an appropriate legal basis and, where necessary, additional notice or consent.

## 6. Legal Basis and Consent

The School shall process personal information only where permitted by applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be supported by:

- Appropriate consent.
- The administration of enrolment or other relevant contractual arrangements.
- Compliance with legal obligations.
- Protection of vital interests.
- Other grounds or exceptions recognised by applicable Malaysian law.

These grounds must not be treated as interchangeable or automatically available for every processing activity.

Where consent is required, the School shall endeavour to ensure that it is informed, specific, appropriately documented, and obtained from a person legally authorised to provide it.

### 6.1 Children’s Information

Where a pupil is legally unable to provide valid consent independently, the School shall obtain authorisation from a parent, legal guardian, or other legally competent person as required.

The School shall not treat a child’s ability to operate a digital account as equivalent to legal capacity to consent to all associated processing.

### 6.2 Essential Educational Processing

Certain information is necessary for enrolment, school administration, educational assessment, attendance, safeguarding, or compliance with applicable requirements.

Where processing is legally necessary for these purposes, withdrawal of optional consent does not automatically require the School to cease all related processing. The School shall explain any relevant consequences and available alternatives where appropriate.

### 6.3 Optional Processing

Optional activities may include public media publication, selected promotional communications, or participation in particular digital initiatives.

Where consent is required for such activities, refusal should not ordinarily disadvantage a pupil’s access to essential education.

## 7. Collection of Information

Information may be collected directly from users or through legitimate school processes. Sources may include:

- Admissions forms.
- Parent registration forms.
- School administrative records.
- Teacher assessments.
- Attendance systems.
- Parent communications.
- Student submissions.
- Consent forms.
- Authorised third-party educational services.
- Technical activity logs.
- Other lawful and relevant sources.

Where personal information is obtained from another source, the School shall comply with applicable notice, consent, and disclosure requirements.

## 8. Student Profiles and Longitudinal Records

The Platform may progressively establish a digital profile documenting a pupil’s educational journey from Standard 1 to Standard 6. This may include academic development, attendance, learning achievements, participation, and Human Values-related educational observations.

The purpose of these records is to support teaching, learning, student development, and appropriate school administration. The School shall endeavour to ensure that such records are accurate, relevant, appropriately contextualised, and accessible only to authorised individuals.

### 8.1 Human Values Observations

Observations relating to Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Nonviolence are intended to support constructive reflection and character development. They must not be treated as permanent judgments concerning a child’s personal worth.

Such observations should not be publicly disclosed or used to unfairly stigmatise pupils.

### 8.2 Learning Support Records

Information relating to educational interventions, remedial support, or learning difficulties may require enhanced confidentiality. Access should be limited to relevant teachers, authorised support personnel, school leadership, and parents or guardians where appropriate.

### 8.3 Historical Records

Certain records may be retained after a pupil completes or leaves the School where necessary for legitimate educational, administrative, legal, or archival purposes. Retention must be governed by an appropriate records retention schedule.

## 9. Access to Personal Information

The Platform uses role-based access arrangements intended to ensure that users can access only information relevant to their authorised responsibilities.

### 9.1 Pupils

Pupils may access their own approved educational information, learning resources, assignments, and other age-appropriate services. They must not access confidential records concerning other pupils.

### 9.2 Parents and Guardians

Parents and guardians may access information concerning children for whom they possess verified authority. Access may be restricted where required by law, safeguarding considerations, or legitimate custody arrangements.

### 9.3 Teachers

Teachers may access information concerning pupils assigned to their classes or educational responsibilities. Access to sensitive information must be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

### 9.4 School Administrators

Authorised administrators may access information necessary for enrolment, records management, communications, and other approved duties.

### 9.5 School Leadership

The Principal and authorised leadership personnel may access information necessary for educational oversight, institutional administration, safeguarding, and compliance.

### 9.6 Board of Governors

Members of the Board of Governors may receive information necessary for legitimate governance responsibilities. Board membership does not automatically confer unrestricted access to individual pupil records or confidential safeguarding information.

### 9.7 Technical Administrators

Technical administrators may access systems and information only to the extent necessary for authorised maintenance, security, troubleshooting, or support. Privileged access should be logged, reviewed, and restricted wherever technically feasible.

## 10. Disclosure of Personal Information

Personal information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary and legally permitted. Recipients may include:

- Authorised school personnel.
- Relevant parents or guardians.
- Approved educational service providers.
- Cloud hosting and technology providers.
- Government departments or educational authorities.
- Professional advisers acting under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- Emergency services or relevant authorities where necessary.
- Other recipients authorised by law or appropriate consent.

The School shall not sell pupils’ personal information to advertisers or data brokers. Personal information must not be disclosed for unrelated commercial marketing without an appropriate legal basis.

- Digital classrooms.
- Document storage.

Certain technology providers may store or process information outside Malaysia. Where cross-border transfers occur, the responsible entity shall comply with applicable Malaysian data protection requirements, including relevant transfer conditions and safeguards.

The services currently in use, and where they process information, are listed in the [Cookies, Analytics & Digital Technologies Policy](/legal/cookies#schedule-a-technologies-in-use).

## 12. Information Security

The School shall endeavour to implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect personal information. These may include:

- Authentication controls.
- Role-based permissions.
- Restricted administrative access.
- Secure communications.
- Encryption where appropriate.
- Access logging.
- Account management procedures.
- Security updates.
- Backup and recovery arrangements.
- Staff confidentiality requirements.
- Periodic permission reviews.
- Incident response procedures.

No digital system can guarantee absolute security. However, reasonable precautions must be taken to reduce foreseeable risks.

Users are also responsible for protecting their credentials and promptly reporting suspected security incidents.

## 13. Personal Data Breaches

A personal data breach may involve unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, loss, or compromise of personal information. Suspected breaches should be reported promptly to the School’s designated contact.

The School shall assess incidents and take appropriate steps to contain, investigate, document, and remediate them.

Where applicable law requires notification to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner, affected individuals, or other authorities, the responsible entity shall comply with the relevant requirements and statutory timelines.

The School may also implement additional safeguarding measures where an incident affects children.

## 14. Data Retention

Personal information shall be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its legitimate purposes, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Different categories of records may require different retention periods. These may include:

- Admissions records.
- Academic records.
- Attendance records.
- Consent records.
- Financial information.
- Staff records.
- Safeguarding documentation.
- Technical logs.
- Alumni records.

The School should establish and maintain a documented retention schedule appropriate to each category. Where information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted, anonymised, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements. Further detail is set out in the [Data Protection, Information Security & Records Retention Policy](/legal/data-protection-retention).

## 15. Data Accuracy and Correction

The School shall take reasonable steps to maintain accurate and current personal information. Parents and guardians are encouraged to notify the School of changes to:

- Contact details.
- Emergency contacts.
- Authorised collection arrangements.
- Relevant medical information.
- Guardianship information.
- Other important family records.

Individuals may request correction of inaccurate personal information in accordance with applicable law. The School may require identity verification and supporting documentation before making material changes.

## 16. Rights of Individuals

Subject to applicable Malaysian law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information. These may include rights to:

- Receive relevant information about processing.
- Request access to personal information.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.
- Limit or object to certain processing where legally permitted.
- Exercise other rights provided by applicable legislation.

Rights may be subject to statutory conditions, exceptions, and verification requirements. Parents or guardians seeking to exercise rights on behalf of children may be required to establish their legal authority.

Requests should be submitted through the School’s designated contact.

## 17. Photographs, Videos, and Student Work

The School may document educational activities through photographs, videos, recordings, and examples of pupil work. Such materials may be used internally for legitimate educational purposes.

Public or promotional use must comply with applicable laws and the School’s [Photography, Video, Student Work & Media Consent Policy](/legal/media-consent). Where appropriate consent is required, the School shall obtain it before publication.

Consent should distinguish between internal educational use and public-facing publication. A parent granting permission for internal school photography does not automatically authorise unrestricted publication on websites or social media.

The School shall take reasonable precautions to avoid disclosing unnecessary identifying information about children.

## 18. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Platform may use cookies and related technologies for legitimate technical purposes. These may include authentication, session management, security, preferences, and service performance.

Optional analytics or other non-essential technologies may be introduced subject to appropriate notice and applicable requirements.

Further information is provided in the [Cookies, Analytics & Digital Technologies Policy](/legal/cookies).

## 19. External Websites and Services

The Platform may contain links to external websites, educational resources, or third-party applications. The School does not automatically control the privacy practices of independent third parties. Users should review the relevant privacy notices where appropriate.

External services intended for pupil use should be assessed and authorised by the School before implementation.

## 20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in law, institutional practices, Platform functionality, or information processing activities.

Material changes shall be communicated through appropriate school channels. Where changes require fresh notice or consent, the School shall undertake the necessary procedures.

The latest effective date and revision date will be displayed on this page.
