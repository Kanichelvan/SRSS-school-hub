## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the SRSS Digital Hub, the official digital platform established to support the educational, administrative, and community activities of Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai (SRSS).

The SRSS Digital Hub (“the Site”, “the Platform”, “the Hub”, “we”, “our”, or “us”, as applicable) is developed under the auspices of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) and operated for the benefit of Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai.

The Platform represents an ongoing digitalisation initiative intended to progressively integrate school communications, teaching and learning resources, student information, academic records, administrative services, parental engagement, and the holistic development of pupils within a secure and accessible digital environment. The inaugural development of the Platform is introduced through SRSS25, an initiative commemorating the School’s 25th anniversary and celebrating its continuing commitment to Human Excellence.

The SRSS Digital Hub is guided by the philosophy of Sathya Sai Educare and the five universal Human Values of Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Nonviolence.

These Terms of Use (“Terms”) govern access to and use of the Platform by visitors, prospective parents, enrolled pupils, parents and legal guardians, teachers, school employees, authorised administrators, alumni, volunteers, and other individuals or organisations accessing its services. By accessing or using the Platform, users agree to comply with these Terms, subject to applicable Malaysian laws and the special provisions governing minors.

Where a user is below the age of eighteen (18), acceptance of these Terms must be undertaken by the user’s parent or legal guardian where legally required. A child’s use of the Platform does not, by itself, establish that the child has entered into a legally binding contract.

These Terms should be read together with the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy), the [Child Protection & Online Safeguarding Policy](/legal/online-safeguarding), the [Intellectual Property, Educational Content & Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/intellectual-property), the [Parental Consent, Guardian Authority & Children’s Digital Participation Policy](/legal/parental-consent), and other applicable policies published by SRSS.

:::box About SSIOM

The SRSS Digital Hub is developed as an initiative of SSIOM to support the digital transformation of Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai.

Subject to applicable third-party intellectual property rights and relevant development arrangements, SSIOM is the designated owner of the Platform’s original software, source code, proprietary design elements, and associated digital infrastructure.

The Platform is operated for the educational and administrative benefit of SRSS. The School’s day-to-day use and administration of the Platform are supervised by the Principal and authorised school personnel. Sekolah Rendah Sathya Berhad retains its responsibilities as the registered governing entity of SRSS.

For the avoidance of doubt, ownership of the Platform’s software does not automatically confer ownership of all educational records, pupil information, confidential school documents, or third-party content stored or processed through the Platform. Parents and legal guardians are responsible for maintaining accurate contact information and exercising appropriate supervision over their children’s use of digital services.

Pupil accounts are intended primarily for educational activities. The School may impose age-appropriate restrictions on communications, content sharing, external applications, and other digital functions.

## 6. Acceptable Conduct and User Responsibilities

All users are expected to uphold the values of Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Nonviolence in their digital interactions. Users must communicate respectfully and refrain from conduct that undermines the dignity, privacy, safety, or wellbeing of others.

The following activities are prohibited:

1. Bullying, harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory abuse.
2. Sharing inappropriate, obscene, exploitative, or harmful material.
3. Attempting to access another user’s account without authorisation.
4. Disclosing confidential pupil, family, or staff information.
5. Uploading malicious software or attempting to compromise Platform security.
6. Impersonating another person or misrepresenting institutional authority.
7. Publishing false or misleading information.
8. Using the Platform for unauthorised commercial solicitation.
9. Collecting or extracting personal information without permission.
10. Circumventing security controls or access restrictions.
11. Misusing photographs, videos, or other material depicting children.
12. Using Platform communications to establish inappropriate relationships with pupils.

The School may investigate suspected misuse and take proportionate action, including restricting access, preserving relevant evidence, notifying parents, initiating disciplinary procedures, or referring serious matters to appropriate authorities. Such action shall be undertaken in accordance with applicable laws and relevant school policies.

## 7. Child Safety and Safeguarding

The safety, dignity, privacy, and wellbeing of children are fundamental considerations in the operation of the SRSS Digital Hub. The Platform must not be used to exploit, groom, threaten, harass, or otherwise endanger a child.

Teachers, staff, volunteers, administrators, and other adults must maintain appropriate professional boundaries when interacting with pupils. Where communication between adults and pupils is necessary for educational purposes, approved school communication channels should be used. Private or informal communication outside authorised channels should not be undertaken in circumstances that compromise safeguarding requirements.

The School may restrict pupil messaging, public posting, external sharing, or other interactive functions according to age, educational necessity, and safeguarding considerations.

Suspected safeguarding concerns should be reported promptly to the Principal or designated safeguarding personnel. Where immediate danger exists, emergency services or appropriate authorities should be contacted without delay.

Further requirements are established in the [Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy](/legal/child-protection) and the [Child Protection & Online Safeguarding Policy](/legal/online-safeguarding).

The School endeavours to maintain accurate and current educational records. However, certain information may remain provisional pending verification, moderation, or formal approval.

### 8.2 Correction of Records

Parents or guardians who identify inaccuracies may submit a correction request to the School. Requests will be reviewed according to the nature of the information, applicable legal requirements, and relevant educational procedures.

### 8.3 Human Excellence Records

The Platform may document pupils’ development in the five Human Values through teacher observations, reflections, achievements, service activities, and other educational experiences. Such records are intended to encourage positive development and meaningful reflection.

They must not be used to publicly rank, shame, stigmatise, or unfairly label children. Human Values observations should be interpreted within their educational context and must not be treated as definitive assessments of a child’s inherent worth or character.
