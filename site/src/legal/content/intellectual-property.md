## 1. Introduction

The SRSS Digital Hub (“the Site”, “the Platform”, “the Hub”, “we”, “our”, or “us”, as applicable) is an evolving digital platform established under the auspices of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) to support the educational, administrative, and community activities of Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai (SRSS).

The Platform is developed as part of the School’s progressive digital transformation, commencing with SRSS25, its inaugural Silver Jubilee initiative, and is intended to support the responsible creation, management, sharing, and preservation of educational resources and digital information.

This Intellectual Property, Educational Content & Acceptable Use Policy (“Policy”) governs the ownership, licensing, permitted use, reproduction, distribution, publication, and protection of intellectual property and digital content associated with the Platform. The Policy applies to pupils, parents, guardians, teachers, employees, administrators, volunteers, alumni, technical contributors, visitors, and other authorised users.

It recognises that different categories of materials may be subject to different ownership arrangements, including original Platform software, school-created educational materials, teacher-developed resources, pupil-created work, third-party publications, photographs, videos, and other digital content.

The Policy is intended to operate consistently with the Copyright Act 1987 (Act 332), other applicable Malaysian laws, relevant contractual arrangements, and the School’s educational responsibilities.

In keeping with the philosophy of Sathya Sai Educare, all users are encouraged to demonstrate Truth through honest attribution, Right Conduct through respect for intellectual property, Peace through responsible communication, Love through constructive sharing, and Nonviolence through the rejection of harmful or exploitative content.

## 2. Ownership of Platform Software and Digital Infrastructure

The original software, source code, proprietary architecture, custom-designed interface elements, original databases and database structures, and other intellectual property specifically developed for the SRSS Digital Hub are intended to be owned by SSIOM, to the extent such rights have been validly created, assigned, or otherwise vested in SSIOM.

Such ownership shall remain subject to applicable intellectual property laws, contributor agreements, open-source licences, and third-party proprietary rights.

The operation of the Platform for SRSS does not, by itself, transfer ownership of SSIOM’s software to Sekolah Rendah Sathya Berhad, the School, individual users, or other parties.

Similarly, SSIOM’s ownership of the software does not automatically confer ownership of personal information, educational records, pupil-created materials, or third-party content stored or processed through the Platform.

### 2.1 Authorised School Use

SRSS is authorised, subject to the applicable institutional arrangements, to use the Platform for legitimate educational, administrative, and school-community purposes.

Such use may include maintaining school records, communicating with parents, facilitating educational activities, managing authorised content, and administering approved digital services.

### 2.2 Software Modifications

Modifications, enhancements, integrations, or extensions to the Platform shall be undertaken only by authorised developers or technical personnel. Ownership of newly developed components shall be determined according to the relevant development and intellectual property arrangements.

### 2.3 Third-Party Software

The Platform may incorporate third-party libraries, software frameworks, application programming interfaces, cloud services, and open-source components. Such components remain subject to their respective licences and intellectual property rights.

Nothing in this Policy shall be interpreted as asserting SSIOM ownership over independently owned third-party software.

## 3. Ownership of School Educational Materials

Educational materials created, commissioned, licensed, or acquired for SRSS may include:

- Lesson plans and teaching resources.
- Worksheets and classroom materials.
- Academic assessments and examinations.
- Educational presentations.
- School handbooks and administrative publications.
- Learning modules.
- Human Values education resources.
- School event materials.
- Digital newsletters and announcements.
- Other educational content.

Ownership of these materials shall be determined according to applicable law, employment arrangements, commissioning agreements, licences, and other relevant circumstances. Materials produced by employees in the course of their employment may be subject to statutory or contractual ownership arrangements.

The mere uploading of educational material to the Platform does not automatically transfer copyright ownership to SSIOM.

### 3.1 Permitted Educational Use

Authorised users may access, download, print, or use educational materials where permitted by the School and the applicable rights holder. Such use should ordinarily be limited to legitimate educational, administrative, or personal learning purposes.

### 3.2 Restrictions on Redistribution

Users must not reproduce, publish, sell, commercially distribute, or otherwise exploit protected educational materials without appropriate permission, except where permitted by applicable law.

School-issued access to a resource does not necessarily confer permission for unrestricted public redistribution.

## 4. Teacher-Created Educational Resources

SRSS recognises the contribution of teachers in developing educational resources and enriching the learning experience of pupils. Teacher-created materials may include original lesson plans, presentations, worksheets, classroom activities, educational recordings, and other instructional resources.

Ownership and permitted use shall depend on applicable copyright law, employment terms, commissioning arrangements, and any relevant institutional policies.

Teachers must ensure that materials uploaded to the Platform do not unlawfully reproduce third-party content. Where externally sourced materials are used, appropriate attribution, permission, or licensing must be observed.

Teachers should not upload confidential school information or pupil records to unauthorised external services.

The School may establish procedures for reviewing, updating, archiving, or withdrawing educational materials made available through the Platform.

## 5. Pupil-Created Work and Intellectual Property

SRSS recognises that pupils may create original works through their educational activities. Such works may include:

- Drawings and paintings.
- Written compositions and poems.
- Creative stories.
- Projects and presentations.
- Digital artwork.
- Audio or video productions.
- Computer programs.
- Other original educational or creative materials.

Copyright ownership of pupil-created work shall be determined in accordance with applicable Malaysian law. The submission of a pupil’s work through the Platform does not automatically transfer ownership to SSIOM, Sekolah Rendah Sathya Berhad, or SRSS.

### 5.1 Educational Use of Pupil Work

The School may receive, store, assess, review, and provide feedback on pupil-created work for legitimate educational purposes. Where appropriate, such work may be displayed within restricted classroom environments or learning portfolios.

### 5.2 Public Publication

Publication of pupil-created work on publicly accessible websites, social media, promotional materials, or external publications may require additional authorisation.

Where the pupil is a minor, the School shall obtain appropriate parental or guardian permission where legally required. The School should also consider the pupil’s wishes, age, maturity, dignity, and welfare.

### 5.3 Attribution

Where appropriate and safe, pupils should receive suitable recognition for their original work. However, the School may limit identifying information to protect a child’s privacy.

### 5.4 Removal Requests

Parents or guardians may request the review or removal of publicly displayed pupil work. Such requests shall be assessed according to applicable law, ownership rights, safeguarding considerations, and the School’s legitimate educational or archival responsibilities.

## 6. Human Values Educational Content

The Platform may provide educational resources relating to Sathya Sai Educare, Education in Human Values, character development, service activities, and the five universal Human Values.

Such materials may originate from SRSS, SSIOM, Sathya Sai Academy for Human Values, the Asia Pacific Sathya Sai Institute for Human Values Education Ltd (APSSIHVE), individual educators, or other authorised sources.

The inclusion of such materials does not automatically establish ownership by SSIOM or SRSS. Materials supplied through educational collaborations shall remain subject to the relevant ownership, permission, attribution, and licensing arrangements.

Users must not alter, misrepresent, or redistribute protected educational materials in ways that infringe the rights of their creators or licensors.

The Platform shall endeavour to present Human Values educational content respectfully and consistently with the School’s educational philosophy.

## 7. School Names, Logos, Emblems and Institutional Identity

The names, logos, emblems, insignia, and other identifying materials associated with SSIOM, Sekolah Rendah Sathya Berhad, SRSS, SRSS Digital Hub, and SRSS25 may be protected by copyright, trademark law, other intellectual property rights, or applicable institutional rules.

Users must not use such identifiers in a manner that falsely suggests official authorisation, endorsement, partnership, or representation. Unauthorised commercial use, misleading reproduction, or modification of institutional identifiers is prohibited.

Permission to use a logo or institutional name for a specific purpose does not automatically authorise use for unrelated purposes.

## 8. User-Submitted Content

The Platform may permit authorised users to submit educational work, comments, forms, photographs, documents, feedback, and other materials. Users are responsible for ensuring that their submissions are lawful, appropriate, and consistent with applicable school policies.

Users must not knowingly upload materials that:

- Infringe intellectual property rights.
- Disclose confidential personal information without authorisation.
- Contain defamatory or malicious statements.
- Promote bullying, harassment, or discrimination.
- Contain inappropriate or exploitative content.
- Compromise the safety or dignity of children.
- Misrepresent another person’s identity or work.
- Introduce malicious code or compromise Platform security.

### 8.1 Limited Permission for Platform Operation

By submitting content through an authorised function, the relevant rights holder grants, or confirms the necessary permission for, the School and authorised service providers to store, reproduce, display, transmit, and process that content to the extent reasonably necessary for the stated educational or administrative purpose.

This permission is limited by applicable law, the scope of the submission, relevant consent arrangements, and any additional conditions communicated to the user. It does not constitute a general transfer of copyright ownership or an unrestricted licence for public promotional use.

Where a minor’s work is involved, any required parental or guardian authorisation must be obtained.

## 9. Acceptable Use of Educational and Digital Content

Users must use the Platform and its content responsibly. Permitted activities may include:

- Accessing approved learning resources.
- Completing school assignments.
- Participating in authorised educational activities.
- Reviewing personal academic information.
- Communicating through approved channels.
- Sharing authorised educational materials.
- Submitting school-related forms and documents.

Users must not use the Platform to distribute unauthorised commercial content, misleading information, confidential records, or material harmful to children.

Access privileges must not be used to obtain information beyond a user’s legitimate educational or administrative responsibilities.

## 10. Prohibited Reproduction and Exploitation

Except where permitted by applicable law or authorised by the relevant rights holder, users must not:

- Copy or redistribute proprietary Platform software.
- Reverse-engineer protected components in circumstances not permitted by law.
- Remove copyright notices or attribution.
- Sell or commercially exploit protected school resources.
- Publish restricted educational materials on unauthorised websites.
- Reproduce pupil-created work without appropriate permission.
- Download or extract confidential school records for unrelated purposes.
- Use institutional branding to impersonate official representatives.
- Circumvent digital access restrictions.
- Misuse copyrighted third-party materials.

Nothing in this Policy restricts rights or exceptions expressly permitted by applicable law.

## 11. Third-Party Intellectual Property

The Platform may contain or link to content owned by external organisations, publishers, educational institutions, software providers, and individual creators. Such content remains subject to its respective terms, licences, and intellectual property rights.

The School shall endeavour to respect applicable third-party rights when incorporating external resources. Users must not assume that materials available through the Platform are free from copyright restrictions.

## 12. Photographs, Videos and Audio Recordings

Photographs, video recordings, audio recordings, and other media may involve overlapping rights and responsibilities, including copyright, privacy, consent, and child safeguarding considerations.

Possession of copyright in a photograph does not automatically authorise unrestricted publication of the image of a child. Similarly, parental consent for publication does not necessarily transfer copyright ownership from the photographer or other rights holder.

Media use shall be governed by this Policy together with the [Photography, Video, Student Work & Media Consent Policy](/legal/media-consent) and applicable privacy and safeguarding requirements.

## 13. Copyright Infringement Complaints

Individuals who believe that their intellectual property rights have been infringed through the Platform may submit a complaint to the School. A complaint should, where reasonably possible, identify:

- The material concerned.
- The relevant intellectual property right.
- The complainant’s relationship to the material.
- The location of the allegedly infringing content.
- Supporting information or documentation.
- Contact details for follow-up.

The School may temporarily restrict access to disputed content while reviewing a complaint. Where appropriate, the School may consult SSIOM, the relevant rights holder, or professional advisers.

The School may remove or restrict content where required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent continued infringement.

## 14. Content Moderation and Removal

The School may review, restrict, correct, archive, or remove content where reasonably necessary to:

- Protect pupils.
- Address intellectual property complaints.
- Prevent unlawful conduct.
- Maintain educational standards.
- Protect confidential information.
- Preserve Platform security.
- Comply with applicable laws.

Content moderation decisions should be proportionate and consistent with relevant school procedures. Where appropriate, affected users may request clarification or review.

The School may preserve relevant evidence where necessary for legal, safeguarding, or administrative purposes.

## 15. Consequences of Policy Violations

Violations of this Policy may result in:

- Removal of unauthorised content.
- Restriction of Platform functions.
- Suspension of accounts.
- Administrative review.
- School disciplinary procedures.
- Referral to appropriate authorities where required.
- Other lawful and proportionate measures.

Actions involving pupils should take account of their age, maturity, circumstances, and educational needs.

Nothing in this Policy prevents rights holders from exercising remedies available under Malaysian law.

## 16. Amendments to This Policy

This Policy may be revised to reflect changes in intellectual property law, educational requirements, software development, Platform functionality, or institutional arrangements.

Material changes shall be communicated through appropriate channels. The latest effective date and revision date will be displayed on this page.

## 17. Governing Law

This Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with the laws of Malaysia, including the Copyright Act 1987 (Act 332), as amended, and other applicable legislation.

Nothing in this Policy shall override statutory intellectual property rights, lawful exceptions, or other non-excludable legal protections.
