# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

## SRSS design decisions

- **Website Architecture v2.0 (user decision, 13 Oct 2026) is definitive.** Three experiences — institutional website, community website and the secure Digital Hub — share one design language but not one navigation. The route map is `src/data/siteMap.js` (one entry per URL, with owner and status; "section" URLs are final but live as anchors on the parent page until verified content justifies a page); `SITEMAP.md` documents it, including the migration checklist and Phase 2 page list. Don’t build a new homepage version or thin pages; refine existing components in their correct homes. Keep URLs lowercase, hyphenated and stable, with a permanent redirect (siteMap `REDIRECTS` + `firebase.json`) whenever one moves; `tests/siteMap.test.mjs` must pass.
- The platform is a dual-plane system (public web presence + secure role-based hub) on a Google Workspace backbone; boundary rules and decisions D1–D6 are in `docs/architecture.md`.
- Public navigation (Architecture v2.0): About SRSS · Learning · Student Life · Join SRSS · Resources · Sign In, plus search and Admissions & Visits, from `src/data/navigation.js`. On desktop the label goes to the landing page and the chevron opens the mega-menu; phones use an accordion drawer. Pages below the homepage show breadcrumbs. Landing pages (`src/pages/SitePages.jsx`) reuse the homepage blocks and FAQ records rather than copying content.
- Use one `/login` entry (`/sign-in` is an alias) and a role-aware `/hub` shell. Parent, student, teacher and admin/staff access is enforced by the data/API layer, not by hidden navigation.
- Google Workspace products support governed SRSS workflows; they are integrations rather than substitutes for clear SRSS pages, permissions, status and confirmation states.

- Use the official SRSS logo only as a compact header and restrained footer brand mark. Do not refer to it as a crest or use oversized emblems, anniversary artwork, decorative school motifs, or unrelated multilingual/religious symbols.
- The public homepage is calm and editorial: solid deep navy, ivory, white, and restrained gold. The interactive Values Garden is the deliberate exception, using the approved vibrant five-value palette; reserve other bright gradients for internal student-hub galleries.
- Homepage photography may feature fictional pupils, but uniforms must follow the real SRSS lavender-and-aubergine-purple uniform. Integrate imagery with softened photographic edges rather than visible rectangular cards.
- The selected visual target is `design-reference.png`, the cinematic navy hero with teacher and pupils, three value pillars, blended learning strip, and four-audience hub row.
- The homepage hero eyebrow is `Since 2002`; the headline is `The End of Education is Character`, with metallic gold reserved for the single word `Character`.
- The school motto is `Human Excellence | Kecemerlangan Insan`; keep it distinct from the educational maxim `The End of Education is Character`.
- Use the exact label `Sai Educare Philosophy`. Values Garden petals must keep their labels comfortably inside the artwork and visibly behave as interactive controls.
- The Values Garden uses five equal radial oval value petals around a true circular centre, with five smaller pointed gold inter-petals placed precisely in the gaps. Keep the established value-to-colour mapping, but render the red, blue, purple, green and orange petals with the vibrant tonal gradients and depth of the supplied 25th-anniversary floral reference. Value names remain bold, white, uppercase radial typography and must stay comfortably inside their petals.
- The homepage contains Our Purpose, an interactive five-leaf Values Garden, a real-photo Life at SRSS carousel, Learning Experience tabs, and a five-audience role navigator.
- The homepage FAQ preview shows five questions and links to `/resources/faq`. FAQ content is database-backed and editable through Admin Content Management.
- The homepage `Find your way · I am a…` role router and the header Sign In link are the public ways into the hubs. Do not reintroduce the lower `One school, many doors` or family-essentials sections.
- The homepage role router has five roles: Parent, Student and Teacher (each marked "Sign in" and opening its hub), Prospective family (→ `/join/admissions`, no login) and Staff (→ Admin Hub). The Admin Hub prioritises communications, people records, admissions, publishing, announcements, events, forms, submissions and operational reminders—not finance-heavy back-office work.
- The attached 500 px circular `SRSS LOGO (2).png` is the current official source of truth for the site logo.
- The Values Garden uses the supplied five-petal SRSS artwork with accessible clickable petal labels; do not return to a generic CSS tree.
- Give newly supplied high-resolution documentary school photographs priority in Life at SRSS. Preserve their originals and use generated imagery only for purpose-built editorial compositions such as the blended hero.
- Preserve the full Principal, Senior Assistant of Academics and pioneer-alumna messages on the public `/about/our-people` editorial page (formerly `/about/voices`). Use the supplied 4:5 portraits, and let the Principal’s message and portrait anchor the homepage Our People feature.
- Keep `/about/our-people` aligned with the approved typography system: Playfair Display for all editorial headings, Open Sans for speech body copy, and Poppins only for labels, navigation and UI.
- Footer copy, Kuala Lumpur address, phone, email and six quick links must remain available and responsive.
- Homepage composition (Architecture v2.0, 13 Oct 2026): announcement bar → hero → welcome (`Welcome to Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai` / `Learning for Life. Growing in Human Excellence.`) with Our Educational Promise (four mission pillars → Vision & Mission) → Learning experience (photo per theme; the Values Garden under Human Values; → Learning) → Find your way → Life at SRSS carousel (six photos; full set in Student Life › Gallery) → Our People (Principal) → Silver Jubilee band (→ `/about/silver-jubilee`) → News & Events with the calendar → five FAQs → short Admissions invitation (full pathway and forms at `/join/admissions`) → Contact & visit → footer.

- Legal pages (user decisions, 13 Oct 2026): eleven policies at `/legal/{slug}` plus an index at `/legal`, built from the SRSS Digital Hub Master Legal Documentation Framework and following the MySAIYa / Sai SMS legal look: Playfair title “SRSS Digital Hub — {Policy}”, one purple date line “Effective date (last revised date): 23rd November 2026” on every page, “Om Sai Ram.” salutation, gold-ruled numbered sections, navy-ruled institution boxes, an amber child protection notice after the introduction, a navy contact panel, and the SSIOM institutional footer. The School-wide Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy (`/legal/child-safeguarding`; other short slugs: `/legal/privacy`, `/legal/terms`, `/legal/acceptable-use`) sits beside the Child Protection & Online Safeguarding Policy (`/legal/online-safeguarding`), which defers to it. Policy text lives in `src/legal/content/{slug}.md`; order, date, status and named contacts live in `src/legal/registry.js`. Phones show Call / Email / Directions buttons and four collapsed accordions. Hub pages keep their own footer with policy links and no admissions links. - Consent (user decisions, 13 Oct 2026): each parent answers one page per child at `/hub/parent/consent` — six yes/no choices built into `src/hub/consentForm.js` plus a Terms/Privacy tick — pre-filled from the school record and applied immediately with no office approval. The office sees one “Waiting for families” list at `/hub/admin/consent` with a reminder button (in-hub notification via the `sendConsentReminders` function) and, for families without an account, a printable pre-filled paper form and a one-step “enter paper answers” panel. Staff confirm a short confidentiality and acceptable-use acknowledgement once. Changing a question’s meaning means raising `CONSENT_FORM_VERSION`.
- Keep digitalisation simple (user decision, 13 Oct 2026): the school admin must not face a tiresome process. Prefer one step over several, reuse records the School already holds, and add approvals or forms only where the law requires them.

## Secure hub implementation decisions

- The product is the **SRSS Digital Hub** (SRSS25 is the launch occasion). The secure hub (`/sign-in`, `/hub/*`) runs on React + Vite with Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Functions, Security Rules) and Google Workspace as the productivity layer. The user chose to keep React + Vite rather than the PRD's Next.js. Setup: `docs/HUB-SETUP.md`; data model: `docs/data-model.md`; requirement status: `docs/requirements.md`.
- `src/auth/permissions.js` is the single source of truth for hub roles (PRD user groups) and the V/C/U/A/R module matrix. Any change must be mirrored in `firestore.rules` / `storage.rules` and pass `npm test` and `npm run test:rules`.
- Roles live only in Firebase custom claims set by `setUserRoles`; access scope lives in server-derived `users/{uid}` (`functions/scope.js`). Per-child records carry `studentId` + `classId`, and hub queries must be constrained so every returned document is readable.
- Hub route metadata (label, module, phase, Google services, screen) lives in `src/hub/routes.js`. Use the shared components in `src/hub/ui.jsx`; the student hub stays deliberately simple (large cards, few choices).
- Human Values are recorded as specific observations, never numeric scores; public showcase needs a moderator and recorded consent; consent records are append-only.
- Hub reads are one-shot (no realtime listeners) to keep Firebase on free allowances.
- Firestore is the system of record for children, guardians, enrolment, attendance, admissions and consent. Applications and consent are hub forms; Google Sheets only receives read-only reports and Google Forms only handles low-risk public intake (user decision, 10 Oct 2026).
- The Silver Jubilee lives at `/about/silver-jubilee` (Architecture v2.0; it grows into a mini-site with its own sub-navigation); the homepage keeps one restrained Silver Jubilee band linking there.
- The Workspace edition is unconfirmed: keep plans edition-neutral until the school confirms (Google for Nonprofits excludes schools).
- Public website images come from the hub image library (Content → Image library): every image needs alt text, and photos showing pupils need the uploader's public-media consent confirmation. Uploads are resized in the browser (max 2,000 px, WebP).
- Any demo role-switching bar is local-demo only (emulators); never ship view switching without sign-in.
- **Extended platform plan (13 Oct 2026):** stages, schema and decisions are in `docs/extended-platform-plan.md`. Stage 1 (academic operations) is built: `/hub/academics` and `/hub/assessments` are shared staff modules (Teaching and Admin hubs; `hub: "staff"` routes in `src/hub/routes.js`). Teachers write plans, tasks and marks only for the class **and subject** they teach (`users.teachingKeys`). Results: teacher submits → Academic Coordinator reviews or returns (never edits marks) → Leadership approves and publishes (decision to confirm with the school) → families see published results; pupils only when the period allows. Firestore is the authoritative record of results; Google Sheets is preparation only (template export, validated import into the draft — no two-way sync).
- Google Workspace actions go through the shared `WorkspaceActionModal` (`src/hub/workspace.jsx`), which returns `{ provider, kind, fileId, title, url, addedBy, addedAt }`. Creating a file opens Google's own editor; documents are edited in Google, not re-implemented in the hub. Never store tokens client-side; per-user OAuth, Drive Picker and API creation arrive with the Stage 5 connection centre behind the same modal. A portalled modal's form must `stopPropagation()` on submit, or the caller's form submits too.
- Firestore batches may make only ~20 rule lookups (`get()`), so client writes of per-pupil records go in chunks (`chunk()` in `src/hub/logic/academics.js`), with the summary/status document written last.
- Any set of five — the human values, Rukun Negara principles, SRSS25 themes and similar — uses the SRSS25 mark's petal colours and glossy finish (`--petal-*` tokens and `--petal-rim` in `styles.css`), always mapped Truth red, Right Conduct blue, Peace purple, Love green, Non-violence orange (the Values Garden mapping) (user decision, 10 Oct 2026).
