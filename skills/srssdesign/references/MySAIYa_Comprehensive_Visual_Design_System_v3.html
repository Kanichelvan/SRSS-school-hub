Section 01

Design Foundation

The strategic visual direction for the MySAIYa Digital Hub.

Design direction

MySAIYa Digital Hub should feel sacred, modern, warm, trustworthy, youth-friendly and organised without becoming childish. It must honour the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba while serving Malaysian young adults through a clear, digital-first experience.

Interface promise

The system should help users learn, participate, serve, reflect, register for events, track progress and stay connected. Every design choice should support clarity, dignity, inclusion and purposeful action.

Sacred-modern

Deep navy, gold accents, calm spacing and rounded digital components.

Calm first

High-energy visuals appear only in campaigns, celebrations or gamified learning.

Human and warm

Real people, natural service moments, respectful imagery and friendly microcopy.

Journey-based

Users should see progress, next steps, learning history and service opportunities.

Section 03

Logo System, Clearspace and Misuse

Mandatory usage rules for MySAIYa and SSIOM brand identity.

Clearspace rule: Define X as the height of the primary emblem or circular mark. Keep at least 0.5X clearspace on all sides. Where the actual emblem cannot be measured, use 24px desktop, 16px mobile, and 20px inside drawers and footers.
Logo variantUse whenAvoid when
Full MySAIYa horizontal lockupDesktop header, official documents, reports, presentation coversVery narrow mobile views or small cards
Logo + MySAIYa Digital Hub brand blockWebsite header, dashboard header, sign-in and registration pagesSmall UI states or favicons
Standalone MySAIYa emblemMobile header, app icon, loading screen, compact sidebarFormal document covers where full identity is required
SSIOM emblemFooter attribution, SSIOM pages, legal/organisation contextAs a replacement for the MySAIYa site identity
White/reversed logoNavy, dark gradient or photo overlay backgroundsWhite, gold or pale backgrounds
Gold premium versionCelebration pages, certificates, 100-year commemorative visualsEveryday navigation and ordinary controls
Do not
Do not stretch, squash, rotate or crop the logo.
Do not recreate the logo using typed text.
Do not change colours, add unapproved glows, shadows or bevels.
Do not place the logo on busy photography without a dark or blurred contrast layer.
Do not use the SSIOM emblem as the main MySAIYa site logo.
Do not use the gold premium version for routine buttons or small UI icons.

Section 12

Photography and Illustration Art Direction

Visual content standards for Malaysian Sai Young Adults and SSIOM service contexts.

Photography direction

Use real, warm, candid images of MySAIYa members, service, bhajans, leadership learning, centre activities and Malaysian community contexts. Prefer dignity, natural light, human connection and clear focus.

Illustration direction

Use minimal flat vector, semi-flat devotional illustration, thin-line sacred geometry, lotus/light/path motifs and mature hand-drawn textures. Avoid childish mascots except in clearly gamified learning modules.

UseAvoid
Real activity photos, warm overlays, respectful cropsGeneric stock photos, artificial corporate poses
Dark navy overlay on hero photos for readabilityText directly on busy images
Dignified service representationPoverty-framing or vulnerable-person exposure
Soft vector illustrations and restrained sacred motifsReligious caricature or random AI styles
Consent-approved portraits and testimonialsIdentifiable minors or service recipients without consent

Section 16

Accessibility Standards

WCAG-conscious requirements for colour, keyboard, forms and media.

AreaRequirement
ContrastNormal text 4.5:1 minimum, large text 3:1 minimum, UI components 3:1 minimum
KeyboardAll navigation, accordions, modals, forms and menus must work without a mouse
FocusVisible focus ring on all interactive elements
FormsLabels must be persistent; errors must be programmatically associated
ImagesMeaningful alt text; decorative images marked empty
VideoCaptions or transcript for important video content
Colour blindnessNever rely on colour alone; pair state with icon and text
Touch targetsMinimum 44px for interactive controls
CarouselsPause control, keyboard controls, no fast auto-rotation
