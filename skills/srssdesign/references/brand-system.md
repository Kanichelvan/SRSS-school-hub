# SRSS Brand System

## Design character

Express SRSS as a premium, values-based Malaysian primary school: calm, luminous, dignified, warm and quietly confident. The public experience should feel breathable and editorial rather than corporate, ornate, childish or dashboard-heavy.

Use one memorable device at a time. For the approved homepage, that device is photographic subjects dissolving naturally into a solid navy hero.

## Typography

- **Playfair Display** — hero statements, page titles, major section headings, editorial quotes. Prefer 600 or 700. Use compact tracking and generous line height.
- **Poppins** — navigation, buttons, tabs, labels, dates, overlines, form labels and compact headings. Prefer 500 or 600.
- **Open Sans** — body paragraphs, descriptions, forms, notices and supporting information. Prefer 400 to 600.

Recommended public scale:

| Role | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hero heading | 44–58 px / 1.06–1.12 | 38–44 px / 1.08–1.14 |
| Section heading | 32–40 px | 28–34 px |
| Card/column heading | 18–22 px | 18–20 px |
| Body | 14–16 px / 1.65–1.8 | 14–16 px / 1.6–1.75 |
| UI label | 11–13 px | 11–13 px |
| Eyebrow | 10–11 px, 0.12–0.18 em tracking | Same |

Do not use decorative scripts. Do not set long body text in Playfair Display.

## Layout and surfaces

- Public content width: 1180–1380 px with 32 px desktop gutters and 18 px mobile gutters.
- Section spacing: 72–100 px for narrative sections; 24–40 px for compact utility bands.
- Radius: 6–12 px for public controls and panels; 16–18 px only for prominent media panels.
- Shadows: use sparingly and softly. Prefer fine rules and surface contrast.
- Borders: `#D1D5DB` or a low-opacity navy. Avoid outlining every block.
- Buttons: solid gold primary, navy/white outline secondary, underlined text tertiary.
- Pills: reserve for real status, filters or compact tags. Do not use pill geometry by default.

## Approved and prohibited combinations

Approved:

- Navy 900 + white
- Navy 900 + Gold 700 for headings or actions
- Gold 700 + Navy 900 text
- White/off-white + Navy 900 text
- Purple 700 + white for compact internal features

Avoid:

- Gold text on white
- Navy 900 text on Navy 700
- Large red alert surfaces
- Teal and orange placed tightly together without neutral separation
- Many strong gradients in one viewport
- Metallic gradients for everyday cards or general navigation
