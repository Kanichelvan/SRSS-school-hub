# SRSS Content and Copy

## Voice

Write with warmth, clarity and quiet conviction. Connect ideals to everyday school life. Sound values-led but not heavy, reverent but not ornate, joyful but not childish, and premium without corporate jargon.

Prefer concrete language: what pupils practise, learn, notice, make, serve or become. Avoid generic claims that could describe any school.

## Signature hero statement

> The future does not walk into our school tomorrow — it sits in our classrooms today.

Approved supporting copy:

> We nurture academic excellence, strong character and compassionate leadership through a values-based education that prepares every child for life.

Alternative signature line:

> Shaping Minds. Nurturing Hearts. Inspiring Lives.

## Footer description

> Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai is a values-based primary school in Malaysia dedicated to nurturing academic excellence, character, and compassionate leadership. Guided by the philosophy of Sathya Sai Educare, we inspire every child to discover the human values of Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Nonviolence, empowering them to become confident learners and responsible global citizens.

## Reusable school descriptions

### Short and elegant

> Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai is more than a school—it is a nurturing community where children grow in knowledge, character, and purpose. Through academic excellence and the practice of universal human values, we prepare young minds to serve society with wisdom, compassion, and integrity.

### Inspirational

> At Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai, education is the journey of drawing out the best within every child. By harmonising the Head, Heart, and Hands, we cultivate learners who excel academically, live by universal human values, and become thoughtful leaders committed to making the world a better place.

### Modern website style

> Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai is a private primary school that integrates Malaysia’s national curriculum with the philosophy of Sathya Sai Educare. We believe education should develop both intellect and character, empowering children to lead with excellence, compassion, integrity, and purpose.

### Premium signature

>

Rooted in the timeless principles of Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Nonviolence, we empower every child to realise their highest potential and become a positive force for humanity.

## Five human values in school life

- **Truth** — sincerity, courage and integrity in word and action.
- **Right Conduct** — punctuality, honesty, responsibility, cleanliness and service.
- **Peace** — quiet reflection, respectful speech and emotional self-control.
- **Love** — kindness in classrooms, care for friends and inclusion across backgrounds.
- **Nonviolence** — gentleness, empathy and care for people, animals and nature.

Do not present the values as decorative slogans. Pair each with a behaviour, story, activity or reflection.

## Action labels

Use specific, calm verbs:

- Explore SRSS
- Admissions & Visits
- Arrange a visit
- Discover our approach
- View all dates
- Find forms and documents
- Go to Parent Hub
- Go to Student Hub
- Read now
- View progress
- Write a reflection

Avoid vague labels such as `Learn more` when a more specific action exists.

## Anniversary usage

Use the anniversary language as a small eyebrow, update-strip message or dedicated campaign feature. Do not let anniversary artwork overpower the school name or public-homepage message.

Suitable forms:

- `SRSS 25th Anniversary Year`
- `Celebrating 25 Years of Human Excellence`
- `2002–2027`
- `Human Excellence · Kecemerlangan Insan`

Verify dates and event details before publishing them as factual content.
