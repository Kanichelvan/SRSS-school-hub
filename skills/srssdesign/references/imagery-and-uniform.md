# SRSS Imagery and Uniform

## Image hierarchy

Prefer authentic SRSS photography when clear, consented and suitable for the layout. Generate fictional school scenes when a high-resolution or compositionally appropriate real image is unavailable.

Never present generated pupils as actual SRSS students. Use descriptive alt text such as `Teacher and pupils reading together in SRSS uniform`, not names or claims about a real event.

## Uniform reference

Match the visible SRSS uniform language:

- lavender short-sleeve collared shirt;
- deep aubergine-purple pinafore or vest;
- matching purple skirt or trousers;
- neat purple tie when appropriate;
- white socks and white or black school shoes;
- restrained name tag or logo/badge area only when a supplied real photograph contains it.

For generated images, omit readable badges, names and logos. Image generators often invent emblems and lettering; explicitly prohibit these.

Do not substitute generic white shirts, navy ties, blazers, tartan or international-school uniforms. Maintain the lavender/aubergine palette across all generated scenes.

## People and representation

- Show a natural multiethnic Malaysian school community, including Malaysian Indian, Malay and Chinese pupils and adults.
- Use primary-school ages approximately 7–12.
- Vary gender and roles naturally.
- Keep expressions warm, candid and attentive rather than exaggerated.
- Do not copy the faces of pupils from supplied references when generating fictional imagery.
- Avoid tokenistic staging and over-polished stock-photo poses.

## Photographic quality

Require:

- premium school-campaign or documentary photography;
- natural Malaysian daylight and believable classroom colour;
- sharp eyes, accurate skin and realistic hands;
- real learning materials and purposeful activity;
- clean focal hierarchy and shallow-to-moderate depth of field;
- high enough resolution for the measured slot.

Reject:

- deformed hands, duplicate pupils, waxy skin or excessive smoothing;
- fake readable book text, signage, badges or school logos;
- mismatched uniform colours;
- generic American or British classroom styling;
- oversaturated rainbow colour or synthetic glow;
- heavy blur that hides the subject.

## Blended-edge hero treatment

Create the blend inside the raster asset:

1. Compose the teacher and pupils on the right two-thirds.
2. Preserve faces, hands and learning materials at full sharpness.
3. Transition the classroom and body edges gradually into pure Navy 950 on the left.
4. Leave approximately 40–48% clean navy negative space for editable HTML copy.
5. Avoid a hard rectangular crop, visible halo, cut-out sticker edge or separate CSS gradient.

The navy field must match the CSS hero background so the image disappears into the surface.

## Learning panorama treatment

Use an extra-wide 3:1 panorama with two or three connected learning moments. Keep it coherent as one environment rather than a collage of boxes. Fade the top and bottom into Off-white `#FBFAF7` within the raster asset.

Suitable recurring themes:

- science dropper or simple experiment;
- reading and collaborative enquiry;
- craft, geometry or model-building;
- planting and care for nature;
- service and inclusive teamwork.

## Asset usage

- `homepage-hero-reference.png` demonstrates the approved navy blend and uniform.
- `student-learning-panorama-reference.png` demonstrates the approved panoramic fade.
- Measure the target component before generating a replacement. Match aspect ratio, focal position and density.
- Do not stretch images or use screenshot crops as production assets.
