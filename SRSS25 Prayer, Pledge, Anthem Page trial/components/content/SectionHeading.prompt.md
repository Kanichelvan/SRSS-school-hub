One-line: the standard SRSS heading block — gold eyebrow, Playfair title, optional gold accent line, Poppins standfirst.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="Sekolah Rendah Sathya Sai · Since 2002"
  title="The End of Education is"
  accent="Character"
  standfirst="At SRSS, education is the journey of drawing out the best within every child."
  tone="navy" size="lg" />
```

Use `accent` for the single gold word or phrase that carries the idea. Centre-align only for full-width band headings on cream; hero and body sections are left-aligned.
