One-line: type-only SRSS lockup for spaces where the crest can't reproduce legibly.

```jsx
<Wordmark variant="srss25" size={40} />
<Wordmark variant="full" tone="onLight" size={22} />
```

Set in Playfair Display. The "25" is always gold. Prefer `Crest` whenever the mark can render at 40px or larger.
