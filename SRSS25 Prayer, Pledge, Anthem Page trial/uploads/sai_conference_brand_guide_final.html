Color Application by Conference Areas

Love Sessions

Devotional bhajans, satsangh sessions, "Celebrating Life With Swami", cultural performances, opening and closing ceremonies. Use warm coral gradients and flowing designs.

Service Sessions

SAI 100 Community Adoption, service wing workshops, E.A.S.E programs, public outreach initiatives. Use vibrant teal with dynamic, action-oriented designs.

Human Values Sessions

APSSIEHV launch, educational workshops, study circles, spiritual sadhana sessions, SSE convocation. Use golden amber with wisdom and enlightenment motifs.

Main Conference

Overall branding, keynote speeches, general materials, name badges, signage, official communications. Use royal purple as the primary foundation color.

Horizontal Layout

∞

Vertical Layout

SSSIO
∞

Use for: Posters, social media posts, tall banner formats

Minimum Sizes

∞

Large: 100px+ width

∞

Medium: 50px width

∞

Small: 24px minimum

✓ DO

  • Maintain clear space around logos
  • Use on high contrast backgrounds
  • Keep proportions intact
  • Use approved color combinations
  • Ensure readability at all sizes

✗ DON'T

  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Use on busy backgrounds
  • Change colors without approval
  • Rotate or skew the elements
  • Use below minimum sizes
