Conference Purpose

To offer a three-day spiritually uplifting global platform for members, devotees, leaders, educators, and changemakers across Zone 4 and beyond, celebrating 100 years of the divine advent of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba through interactive learning, empowering workshops, enduring fellowships, meaningful connections, heartfelt devotion, vibrant cultural expressions, and a profound commitment to living with values.
